EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================
A scheduled quarterly inspection of the Crude Distillation Unit
(CDU) heat exchangers and associated pipework was conducted on
2026-08-15. Three significant findings requiring corrective
action before the next operational cycle were identified.
One finding is classified HIGH severity and must be addressed
within 7 calendar days per OISD Standard 118 Section 4.3.

================================================================
SECTION 1 — TEMPERATURE ANOMALY
================================================================
Finding ID : F-001
Component  : Pre-heat exchanger HX-401 (shell side outlet)
Location   : Column A, Row 3, West Bay
Severity   : HIGH
Observation:
    Infrared thermography and thermocouple readings at HX-401
    shell-side outlet consistently recorded 312 °C against a
    design maximum of 290 °C — a deviation of +22 °C (7.6%).
    Prolonged operation above design temperature risks stress
    corrosion cracking of the carbon steel shell.
Recommended Action:
    Reduce throughput to 85% capacity and schedule tube bundle
    inspection. Replace fouled tube internals if fouling index
    exceeds 0.0003 m²·K/W. Refer OISD-118 Cl. 4.3 for
    mandatory approval workflow prior to restart.
Status: OPEN — corrective action required before next run.

================================================================
SECTION 2 — PRESSURE VARIANCE
================================================================
Finding ID : F-002
Component  : Overhead condenser line OC-12 (inlet flange)
Location   : Overhead deck, North side
Severity   : MEDIUM
Observation:
    Operating pressure at the OC-12 inlet flange averaged
    18.4 bar(g) over the 72-hour observation window against
    a design operating pressure of 17.5 bar(g). The +0.9 bar
    variance (5.1%) is within the pressure safety valve (PSV)
    set-point of 21.0 bar(g) but exceeds the +3% instrument
    tolerance band specified in the process data sheet.
    Possible root cause: partial blockage in the overhead
    vapour line downstream of the condenser.
Recommended Action:
    Inspect and clean the overhead vapour line. Re-calibrate
    pressure transmitter PT-4012. Re-validate against P&ID
    drawing MRPL-U4-PID-CDU-014 Rev. C.
Status: OPEN — inspection within 30 days.

================================================================
SECTION 3 — CORRODED GASKET
================================================================
Finding ID : F-003
Component  : Pump suction line flange PL-22 / PL-23
Location   : Ground level, East pump skid
Severity   : LOW
Observation:
    Visual inspection of the spiral-wound gasket at the
    PL-22/PL-23 flange joint revealed surface oxidation
    (red-brown scale) consistent with atmospheric moisture
    ingress. No active leakage detected. Gasket material:
    304 SS / flexible graphite (Flexitallic CG style).
    Expected remaining life at observed corrosion rate:
    approximately 8–12 months.
Recommended Action:
    Replace gasket at next planned shutdown (estimated
    Q1 2027). Apply anti-corrosion coating to exposed
    bolt/nut surfaces. Log in the preventive maintenance
    tracker (PMT-2024-GL-PL22).
Status: OPEN — planned maintenance (non-urgent).

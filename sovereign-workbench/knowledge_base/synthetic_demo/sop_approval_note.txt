SOVEREIGN WORKBENCH REFERENCE DOCUMENT
Standard Operating Procedure — Inspection Finding Approval Note
Document ID : SOP-OPS-2024-017
Revision    : B
Effective   : 2024-09-01
Owner       : Operations Excellence Group

================================================================
1. PURPOSE
================================================================
This SOP defines the mandatory workflow for issuing an Approval
Note (AN) following a scheduled or unscheduled inspection of any
process unit operating under OISD Standards 118 or 141.

An Approval Note is the formal instrument that authorises (or
conditionally authorises) restart of a process unit after an
inspection finding has been reviewed and dispositioned.

================================================================
2. SCOPE
================================================================
Applies to all process units in the refinery complex, including:
  - Crude Distillation Units (CDU)
  - Vacuum Distillation Units (VDU)
  - Hydrotreaters and Hydrocrackers
  - Utility systems (boilers, cooling towers, flare systems)

================================================================
3. REGULATORY BASIS
================================================================
OISD Standard 118, Section 4.3 — "Inspection and Corrective
Action Closure" mandates that no process unit may be restarted
after a HIGH-severity finding until a signed Approval Note is
issued by the Chief Inspector or designated authority.

Failure to obtain an Approval Note before restart constitutes a
non-compliance event and must be reported to the Plant Safety
Officer within 24 hours (OISD-118 Cl. 9.2).

================================================================
4. APPROVAL NOTE — MANDATORY SECTIONS
================================================================
Every Approval Note issued under this SOP must contain:

4.1  HEADER INFORMATION
     - Report Reference (from the Inspection Report)
     - Facility / Unit identification
     - Inspection date
     - Approval Note serial number (auto-assigned by ERP)

4.2  FINDINGS SUMMARY TABLE
     A verbatim copy of the inspection findings table, with
     columns: Finding ID, Description, Severity, Status,
     Disposition (Accept / Reject / Conditional Accept).

4.3  CORRECTIVE ACTION REGISTER
     For each OPEN finding, the Approval Note must record:
     - Agreed corrective action (verbatim from inspection report
       or amended by the Chief Inspector)
     - Responsible engineer name and employee ID
     - Committed completion date (must comply with timelines
       specified in OISD-118 Cl. 4.3)

4.4  CONDITIONS FOR RESTART (if Conditional Accept)
     List all operating restrictions that must be maintained
     until corrective actions are closed, e.g.:
       "HX-401 — operate at ≤ 85% throughput until tube bundle
       replaced. Maximum shell-side outlet temperature: 295 °C."

4.5  CITATION OF RETRIEVED STANDARDS
     The Approval Note must cite the specific SOP, clause, and
     regulatory reference relied upon. Format:
       "[Source: <document name>, <clause>]"
     This field is required for audit traceability.

4.6  AUTHORITY SIGNATURES
     - Chief Inspector (mandatory for HIGH findings)
     - Plant Safety Officer (mandatory for MEDIUM/HIGH findings)
     - Plant Manager (mandatory for HIGH findings or unit restart)

================================================================
5. WORKFLOW STEPS
================================================================
Step 1: Inspector submits Inspection Report to Chief Inspector
        within 24 hours of inspection completion.
Step 2: Chief Inspector reviews within 48 hours and classifies
        each finding.
Step 3: For HIGH findings — convene a Corrective Action Review
        Board (CARB) within 7 calendar days.
Step 4: Draft Approval Note using this SOP template (Section 4).
Step 5: Obtain all required signatures (Section 4.6).
Step 6: Upload signed Approval Note to the DMS under folder:
        OPS / INSPECTION / APPROVAL_NOTES / <Year> / <UnitID>
Step 7: Notify Plant Control Room that restart is authorised.
Step 8: Close corrective actions in PMT system within agreed
        timelines. Verify closure with follow-up inspection.

================================================================
6. TIMELINES (OISD-118 REFERENCE)
================================================================
Severity | CARB Convene | Corrective Action Deadline
---------+--------------+----------------------------------
HIGH     | 7 days       | Before restart (mandatory hold)
MEDIUM   | 30 days      | Within 30 days of inspection
LOW      | 90 days      | At next planned shutdown
INFORMATION | N/A       | Log in PMT; no deadline
